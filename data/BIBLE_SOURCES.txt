The Applied Word Podcast — Bible text sources

BSB — Berean Standard Bible
Public Domain. Source already bundled with this project from BereanBible.com.

ASV — American Standard Version (1901)
Public Domain. Normalized for the app from the Scrollmapper Bible Databases plain-text ASV edition, whose underlying ASV text is public domain.
Reference source: eBible.org identifies the ASV (1901) as Public Domain.

YLT — Young's Literal Translation
Public Domain. Normalized for the app from the Scrollmapper Bible Databases plain-text YLT edition.
Reference source: eBible.org identifies Young's Literal Translation as Public Domain.

KJV — King James Version
Public Domain in the United States. Normalized for the app from the Scrollmapper Bible Databases plain-text KJV edition.
Reference source: eBible.org identifies the KJV as public domain in the USA and notes special Crown rights concerning publication/import in the United Kingdom.

Each app data file uses one verse per line in the form:
Book Chapter:Verse<TAB>Verse text

The app imports a translation into IndexedDB only after the user taps its Download button.
